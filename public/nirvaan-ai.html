The Challenge

The Challenge in MPLAD Monitoring

Thousands of MPLAD-funded projects run simultaneously across the country. At this scale, manually verifying spend, progress and irregularities becomes nearly impossible — leaving gaps where inefficiency and fraud can hide.

Data Complexity

Large volumes of project and financial information across states and constituencies.

Hidden Anomalies

Unusual patterns in spending or timelines can be difficult to identify manually.

Fraud Risk

Suspicious activities may remain buried within large, disconnected datasets.

Inefficiency

Delays and resource inefficiencies can be difficult to track and act on in time.
